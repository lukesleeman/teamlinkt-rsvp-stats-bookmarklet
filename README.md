## Features

- **By event type**: for each kind of session, a turnout chart over time (hover a dot for the date, event name and count) and a leaderboard of who attended most
- **By member**: a table of every member's attendance per kind of session, sortable by any column; click a member for their sessions per month
- **Copy** buttons that copy either table in a format that pastes straight into Excel or Google Sheets
- **Export CSV**: the raw data, one row per event per member with their RSVP (Going, Maybe, Not Going, No Response)
- **Date range**: last 3, 6 or 12 months, or any custom range within the last 12 months
- **Fast repeat runs**: RSVPs for past events are saved in your browser, so only new events are fetched next time

"Attended" means the member RSVPed **Going** or **Maybe**. TeamLinkt doesn't record who actually turned up.

## Usage

1. Log in to TeamLinkt and pick your team
2. Click the bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar
3. The first run fetches every past event from the last 12 months, which takes about a minute. Later runs take a few seconds
4. Use **⟳ Reload** to throw away the saved data and fetch everything again

### How events are grouped

Events are grouped automatically by name:

- Cancelled events are left out
- Names are cleaned up: emoji, capital letters, plurals and anything after a `:` or ` - ` are ignored, so "Pango Gym - Wednesday" and "Pango Gym - Wednesdays" end up together
- A name that runs at several regular times is split by weekday and time, e.g. "Dragon Boat Training – Tue 6:00pm" and "– Sat 9:30am"
- A one-off event held instead of a regular session (same place, weekday and time, on a day that session didn't run) counts towards that session, e.g. a River Run in place of Saturday training. Meetings never do
- A one-off whose name ends in the same word as a group joins it, e.g. "Dragon Boat Camp 1" joins "Training Camp"
- Anything else goes into **Other**

### Privacy

Everything runs in your browser using your own TeamLinkt login. The saved RSVPs stay in this browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere else.
